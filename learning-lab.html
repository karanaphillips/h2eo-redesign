How it works

Every course follows the same four-step loop — from first concept to finished project.

1

Read a short lesson

Each unit opens with a focused reading — just the concept you need for the lab ahead, without detours.

2

Check your understanding

A quick knowledge check confirms you caught the key idea before you write a line of code or solve a problem.

3

Solve labs as you go

Hands-on labs run and check your work right in the browser — nothing to install. You see whether it works immediately.

4

Build your course project

Each lab adds the next piece to one continuing project you carry through the entire course from start to finish.
