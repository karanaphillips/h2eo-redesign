Accessibility Statement

Here to Educate Others, LLC (H2EO)  ·  Last updated: July 26, 2026

We are committed to making H2EO accessible to everyone. If you encounter a barrier, please tell us — email hello@heretoeducateothers.com and we will respond within 5 business days.

Our Commitment

Here to Educate Others, LLC believes that accessible design is good design — and that it's especially important for an educational organization serving diverse learners. We are committed to ensuring that our website and platform are usable by people with a wide range of abilities, including those who use assistive technologies.

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

What We've Built In

The following accessibility features are implemented across our website:

  • ⌨️
    Keyboard navigation — all interactive elements (navigation, forms, buttons) are accessible without a mouse, in a logical tab order. Done
  • 🔗
    Skip to main content — a skip link appears at the top of every page when keyboard focus lands on it, allowing users to bypass the navigation. Done
  • 🏷️
    Descriptive labels & ARIA — form fields have visible labels, images have descriptive alt text (or are marked decorative), and interactive components use ARIA roles and attributes. Done
  • 👁️
    Focus indicators — all focusable elements display a clearly visible focus ring that meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements. Done
  • 📱
    Touch target sizes — buttons and links are sized to a minimum of 44×44 CSS pixels to meet mobile accessibility standards. Done
  • 🔤
    Readable typography — body text is set in Inter at a base size of 1rem. Headings follow a logical hierarchy (h1 → h2 → h3). Line spacing supports readability. Done
  • 📐
    Responsive layout — the site is fully usable at any screen size, from mobile phones to wide desktop monitors, without horizontal scrolling. Done
  • 🎨
    Color contrast — text and UI elements are designed to meet or exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text). Done
  • 📣
    Live regions — toast notifications and form feedback are announced to screen readers via ARIA live regions. Done

Known Limitations

We are actively working to improve accessibility. The following known limitations are on our roadmap:

  • Hero LMS platform: Portions of the Hero LMS are still being reviewed for full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. If you encounter a specific barrier, please contact us.
  • Assessment experience: The Academic Superpowers™ assessment is being audited for screen reader compatibility. We can provide an alternative format on request.
  • PDF resources: Any downloadable PDF guides are not yet fully tagged for assistive technology. We can provide accessible alternative formats on request.
  • Video content: Any video content added to the platform will be captioned. Videos added prior to this commitment may not yet have captions.

Tested Assistive Technologies

Our website has been reviewed using the following assistive technologies:

  • VoiceOver (macOS and iOS)
  • NVDA (Windows)
  • Keyboard-only navigation (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
  • Browser zoom up to 200%

Requesting Accommodations

If you need content, services, or assistance in an alternative format — including large print, audio, or a different file format — please contact us. We are committed to finding a solution that works for you.

We can provide:

  • Assessment results in plain text or alternative formats
  • Course materials in accessible document formats on request
  • Real-time captioning or other accommodations for live sessions when arranged in advance
  • Alternative navigation pathways through content where automated tools are not sufficient

Report an Accessibility Issue

If you find something on our site that creates a barrier for you — a missing label, a confusing interaction, contrast that's too low, or anything else — please tell us. Specific feedback is incredibly helpful.

When you contact us, it helps to include:

  • The URL of the page where you encountered the issue
  • A description of the barrier
  • The assistive technology and browser you were using (if applicable)

Email: hello@heretoeducateothers.com
Subject line: Accessibility Issue

We will acknowledge your message within 2 business days and aim to resolve or work around the issue within 10 business days.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility complaint, you may contact the U.S. Access Board at www.access-board.gov or your regional civil rights enforcement agency.

Our Ongoing Work

Accessibility is not a one-time project — it is an ongoing commitment. We conduct periodic reviews of our website and platform, incorporate accessibility into our design and development process, and welcome feedback from our users as one of our primary sources of improvement.
