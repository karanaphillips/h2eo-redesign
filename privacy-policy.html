1. Who We Are

Here to Educate Others, LLC ("H2EO," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at heretoeducateothers.com and the Hero LMS platform. We provide educational services, learning assessments, coaching, and digital learning tools.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website, take an assessment, enroll in a program, or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and any message you send through our contact forms or when booking a consultation.
  • Assessment responses: Your answers to the Academic Superpowers™ assessment and the resulting archetype profile.
  • Account information: Username, email address, and password when you create a Hero LMS account.
  • Payment information: If you purchase a service or course, payment details are processed directly by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store your full card number.
  • Communications: Emails, messages, and feedback you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, links clicked, and general navigation patterns.
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, and screen size (used to optimize display).
  • IP address: Used to estimate your general geographic region for service delivery. We do not use it to identify you individually.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, financial account details beyond what's needed for payment processing, or any biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our educational services and platform.
  • To generate and display your Academic Superpowers™ profile and learning recommendations.
  • To respond to your inquiries, book consultations, and provide support.
  • To send service-related communications (appointment confirmations, course updates).
  • To send the H2EO newsletter and educational updates — only if you opt in.
  • To process payments for courses and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We will not use your information for purposes materially different from those listed above without notifying you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate H2EO. Each is bound by its own privacy commitments:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Stripe — processes payments securely. We do not receive or store full payment card details.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts our public website files.
  • Vercel — hosts the Hero LMS platform.
  • Supabase — stores Hero LMS account data and assessment records.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights and safety of H2EO, our users, or the public.

Business transfers

If H2EO is ever acquired, merged, or undergoes a significant change in ownership, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance and give you the opportunity to delete your account.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in the United States. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and access controls on our systems.

Assessment results stored in your browser's local storage are under your direct control — clearing your browser data removes them. Account data is stored in Supabase with authentication-protected access.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately at hello@heretoeducateothers.com.

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion at any time (see Your Rights below).

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our public website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. The Hero LMS platform uses session cookies to keep you logged in. These are essential to the service and do not track you across other websites.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or behavioral tracking tools.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information and account.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of marketing: Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@heretoeducateothers.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

H2EO serves K-12 students through educators and parents — we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Our K-12 assessments and services are designed to be used with parent or educator oversight.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information without parental consent, please contact us at hello@heretoeducateothers.com and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide a more prominent notice — such as an email to registered users or a banner on the site. Your continued use of H2EO after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
